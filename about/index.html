About

Hi, I'm Josh McKinney, a software engineer from Australia, living in Seattle. I've spent 20+ years building software for e-commerce, healthcare, finance, transportation, and government.

What I do

I work on the systems other engineers depend on: backend services, platforms, security, and the tools developers use every day. At Amazon and AWS I built and ran authentication and authorization services, including an OAuth / OpenID Connect service securing 80K+ service-to-service connections. At OpenAI I worked on Codex, OpenAI's AI coding agent, across its terminal UI, login, connectivity, and reliability.

The things I care about most are clear interfaces, good docs, and tools that are straightforward to operate.

Now

I co-maintain Ratatui, the Rust terminal UI library, and build tools and widgets around it. I'm looking for my next role in backend, platform, security, or developer tooling. If that sounds like a fit, get in touch.

Before that

Before Amazon I spent a decade consulting in Australia and the US: budget systems for governments from Tennessee to Vanuatu, banking and payments apps, and a lot of performance work. The details are on my resume.

Outside work

I make music with synthesizers (SoundCloud) and tinker with hardware like the Raspberry Pi Pico. How I got started with computers is in my geek origin story.
