Ratatuiv0.30.2

Co-maintainer of the standard Rust terminal UI library. I've worked on Ratatui since the 2023 fork from tui-rs.

  • Day-to-day maintainer work: API design and review, contributor PR review, releases, triage, docs, and community support.
  • Designed much of the everyday API: Layout::areas, the Text, Line, and Span ergonomics, and ratatui::run.
  • Built much of ratatui.rs, and maintain the crates underneath: kasuari, instability, line-clipping.
  • Started tui-widgets and the other widgets below.
