## What I do

I work on the systems other engineers depend on: backend services, platforms, security, and the
tools developers use every day. At Amazon and AWS I built and ran authentication and authorization
services, including an OAuth / OpenID Connect service securing 80K+ service-to-service connections.
At OpenAI I worked on Codex, OpenAI's AI coding agent, across its terminal UI, login, connectivity,
and reliability.

The things I care about most are clear interfaces, good docs, and tools that are straightforward to
operate.

## Now

I co-maintain [Ratatui](https://ratatui.rs), the Rust terminal UI library, and build
[tools and widgets around it](/projects/). I'm looking for my next role in backend,
platform, security, or developer tooling. If that sounds like a fit, [get in touch](#contact).

## Before that

Before Amazon I spent a decade consulting in Australia and the US: budget systems for governments
from Tennessee to Vanuatu, banking and payments apps, and a lot of performance work. The details
are on my [resume](/resume/).
